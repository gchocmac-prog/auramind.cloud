\# Auramind Website Prototype Brief



\## Company



Auramind is a Malaysia-based AI infrastructure delivery partner serving Southeast Asia.



The company helps enterprises, investors and project stakeholders turn AI infrastructure requirements into structured delivery workstreams.



\## Core Positioning



AI Infrastructure, Delivered Across Southeast Asia.



Auramind is execution-led, vendor-neutral and regionally connected.



\## Main Service Paths



1\. AI Infrastructure Delivery

\- Infrastructure planning

\- Procurement and supply coordination

\- Deployment and integration

\- Private AI systems

\- Managed operations

\- Lifecycle support



2\. Regional Resource Integration

\- Project and land sourcing

\- AIDC site readiness screening

\- Power and connectivity review

\- Stakeholder coordination

\- Early opportunity structuring



Do not advertise GPU rental unless confirmed later.



\## Visual Direction



Circular retro-futuristic enterprise design.



Use:

\- Large circles, arcs and cropped circular forms

\- White and near-white backgrounds

\- Strong black structural forms

\- Controlled silver reflective gradients

\- Small yellow highlights

\- Generous whitespace

\- Premium but practical enterprise feeling



Colors:

\- White: #fdfdfd

\- Silver gray: #aeb1b8

\- Yellow: #f8e46a

\- Black: #000000



Typography:

\- Shrikhand only for selected expressive display words

\- Use a clean sans-serif such as Manrope for navigation, body copy and UI



Avoid:

\- Generic blue AI visuals

\- Cyberpunk effects

\- Excessive glowing particles

\- Dashboard-heavy layouts

\- Too many service cards

\- Full-screen Kuala Lumpur background photographs

\- Excessive metallic effects
