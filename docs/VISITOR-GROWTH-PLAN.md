# Plan: Attract more visitors to auramind.cloud

A practical, phased plan to increase traffic and qualified leads for Auramind AI (AI infrastructure management, server rentals, IDC operations).

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (quick wins, 0–4 weeks)

### 1.1 SEO basics
- **Title and meta** – Ensure every page has a unique `<title>` and meta description (you already set “Auramind AI” globally; consider page-specific titles for Services, About, Blog, Contact for search).
- **Headings** – Keep one clear H1 per page; use H2/H3 for sections so search engines understand structure.
- **URLs** – Keep clean URLs (you already have `/services/`, `/blog/`, `/about-us/`, `/faqs/`).
- **Sitemap** – Add a `sitemap.xml` listing all main pages and blog posts; submit in Google Search Console (and Bing if useful).
- **Google Search Console** – Verify the domain, submit sitemap, fix any crawl errors.

### 1.2 Content that pulls search traffic
- **Blog** – Publish 2–4 **original** posts aimed at keywords your buyers use, e.g.:
  - “AI server rental: what to look for”, “Managed AI infrastructure vs in-house”, “GPU server options for ML training”, “Choosing an AI data center partner in Malaysia/APAC”.
- **FAQs** – Expand FAQs with real questions (pricing model, SLAs, regions, GPU types). Turn each into a short, clear answer; this helps both SEO and conversion.
- **Services pages** – Add a short “Who it’s for” or “Use cases” (e.g. research labs, fintech, media) and 2–3 bullet benefits per service.

### 1.3 News quality (done)
- Promotional posts are filtered out of the RSS feed so the Tech News section stays editorial and relevant.

---

## Phase 2: Reach and credibility (1–3 months)

### 2.1 Backlinks and mentions
- **Partners and clients** – Ask existing clients or partners for a short quote and, if possible, a link to auramind.cloud (e.g. “Infrastructure by Auramind AI”).
- **Directories and listings** – Add the company to relevant B2B/tech directories (e.g. Clutch, G2, regional tech/startup directories, industry associations).
- **Guest posts / bylines** – Offer to write one guest article for a tech or AI blog (e.g. “How we run AI infrastructure for X”) with a link back to your site.
- **Press** – One press release or short “Launch” / “Partnership” announcement; share with local tech press and newswires.

### 2.2 Social and community
- **LinkedIn** – Post 1–2 times per week: short takes on AI infra, tips, company updates, links to your blog or services. Encourage team to share.
- **Twitter/X** – If you use it: share blog posts, tech news, and industry comments; engage with AI/ML and infra accounts.
- **Newsletter** – Add a signup on the site (footer or blog). Send a monthly or biweekly “AI infra digest”: 2–3 links to your posts + 2–3 curated external reads. Builds returning visitors and leads.

### 2.3 Local and regional
- **Google Business Profile** – Create/claim and complete with address, phone, hours, short description, link to site. Encourage a few genuine reviews.
- **Local search** – Use “AI infrastructure Malaysia” / “GPU server rental Kuala Lumpur” (etc.) in one dedicated page or section and in blog titles where accurate.

---

## Phase 3: Scale and convert (3–6 months)

### 3.1 Content system
- **Editorial cadence** – Aim for 1–2 new blog posts per month (how-to, comparisons, trends, case-style stories).
- **Topics** – Prioritise “problem” and “comparison” queries: “AI server rental cost”, “managed vs self-managed AI infrastructure”, “best GPU for LLM training”.
- **Internal links** – Link from blog posts to relevant service pages and contact; link from homepage and services to key blog posts.

### 3.2 Conversion on-site
- **Clear CTAs** – One main CTA per page (“Get support”, “Request a quote”, “Book a call”) above the fold or after key sections.
- **Contact** – Keep the form and email/phone visible; add a short “We reply within 24 hours” or similar if true.
- **Trust** – Add 2–3 short testimonials or “As used by” logos; consider a simple “Partners” or “Technologies we work with” strip.

### 3.3 Paid (optional)
- **Google Ads** – If budget allows: run search campaigns on terms like “AI server rental”, “GPU server Malaysia”, “managed AI infrastructure” with landing pages = Services or Contact.
- **LinkedIn Ads** – Target job titles (e.g. CTO, ML lead, Head of Infrastructure) and companies in sectors you serve.
