## Content you can add

### 1. **Trust and social proof**
- **Testimonials / quotes** – 2–3 short client or partner quotes on Home or About Us (e.g. “Auramind helped us scale our training cluster with minimal downtime”).
- **Partners / certifications** – A “We work with” or “Certified by” strip (logos + optional one-line text) on Home or About.
- **Case studies** – Optional standalone page or section: “How we helped [industry/use case]” (e.g. startup, enterprise, research) with problem → solution → outcome.

### 2. **About Us**
- **Team / leadership** – Short “Our team” or “Leadership” section with names, roles, and optional photos.
- **Story / timeline** – A few lines or a short timeline: “How we started” or “Our journey”.
- **Values or differentiators** – 3–4 bullets or icons (e.g. “24/7 support”, “GPU expertise”, “On-prem and cloud”).

### 3. **Services**
- **Pricing / plans** – Even high-level: e.g. “Starter / Pro / Enterprise” or “Discovery call → Custom proposal” (no need to publish actual prices).
- **Use cases** – Short “Ideal for” or “Use cases”: research, fintech, media, enterprise AI, etc., with links to relevant service or contact.
- **Technology stack** – Brief “Technologies we work with” (e.g. NVIDIA Hopper/Blackwell, common frameworks, clouds) on Services or a dedicated “Technology” subsection.

### 4. **Blog**
- **Original posts** – In addition to TECH NEWS (RSS), add 1–2 original posts (e.g. “Getting started with AI server rental”, “When to choose managed AI infrastructure”). Reuse the same card/layout as existing blog posts.
- **Categories** – You already have categories (e.g. technology-trends, ai-infrastructure). Add a “Getting started” or “Best practices” category and tag new posts accordingly.
- **Newsletter** – Optional CTA: “Get AI insights in your inbox” with email signup (e.g. Mailchimp, ConvertKit) on Blog or footer.

### 5. **Contact / conversion**
- **Contact Us** – You already have form + FAQs. Optional: repeat “Get in Touch” (email, phone, address) or a small map snippet so Contact feels complete even if users don’t scroll FAQs.
- **Clear CTAs** – Ensure each main page has one primary CTA (e.g. “Get Support”, “Book a call”, “Request a quote”) in nav or hero.

### 6. **Operational / legal**
- **Terms of Service & Privacy Policy** – Footer already links to `#terms-of-service` and `#privacy-policy`. Add real pages (e.g. `terms/index.html`, `privacy/index.html`) or anchors with content so the links don’t 404.
- **Service status / SLA** – Optional simple “Service status” or “Uptime” page if you want to communicate reliability (e.g. “Infrastructure status” with last updated date).

### 7. **Resources**
- **Downloads** – e.g. “AI infrastructure checklist”, “Server sizing guide” (PDF) with a short form or direct link.
- **Events / webinars** – If you run or join events, a small “Events” or “Webinars” section or page with next event + link to register.

---

1. Add **2–3 testimonials** or **partner logos** on the homepage.
2. Add **1–2 original blog posts** and link them from Blog and (if relevant) Services.
3. Create **Terms** and **Privacy** pages so footer links work.
4. Add a short **“Why Auramind”** or **values** block on About Us (3–4 bullets).
5. Add a **newsletter signup** in the footer or on the Blog page.
